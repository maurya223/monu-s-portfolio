Work with me: how we build your application

A clear path from your problem to a deployed system. You see working output early, not just at the end.

01

Discovery

We define the problem, who uses it and what "done" means. I also check whether you need simple automation, an AI feature, or a real agent.

02

Plan on paper

Data sources, architecture, API contracts and the trade-offs (cost, speed, accuracy), written down and agreed before any code.

03

Working prototype

A thin end-to-end version on your real data, so you can react to something that runs.

04

Build and integrate

Backend, LLM or RAG pipeline, and connections to your tools, with retries, error handling, logging and human-approval steps where needed.

05

Test and evaluate

Output checked against real examples, edge cases and failure paths, including hallucination control for LLM features.

06

Deploy and support

Shipped on your cloud or mine, with docs and a handover. I stay available for fixes and improvements after launch.
